**Jiwon Shon** and **Boyoon Kim** have been selected for the **2026 K-BTIP Global Bridge Training Program** (핵심인재 글로벌 브릿지 연수 프로그램) supported by the **Korea Health Industry Development Institute (KHIDI, 한국보건산업진흥원)**!

They will participate in a 1-month research training program at the **University of Basel** in Switzerland.

Congratulations to Jiwon and Boyoon on this wonderful achievement and exciting opportunity!
